Our Commitment to Your Safety & Well-being

🛡️

Secure Matching & Vetted Drivers

An intelligent algorithm pairs you with drivers based on proximity, availability, and a preference for zero reported incidents. All drivers undergo rigorous identity verification, background checks, and mandatory inclusivity training, ensuring a 'Verified Safe' experience.

🚨

In-App Emergency SOS & Support

A persistent, customizable SOS button activates with a 3-second press-and-hold, instantly alerting emergency contacts, our 24/7 support team, and local transgender community hotlines with your ride details and GPS location.

💬

Anonymous & Detailed Feedback System

Provide anonymous, yet traceable, detailed feedback after every ride. Report issues like misgendering or inappropriate conduct with specific categories, free-text, and optional media, with full transparency on investigation status and resolution.

🔒

Customizable Privacy & Identity Settings

Control how your preferred name, pronouns, and profile picture are displayed. Utilize private instructions for discreet pick-up/drop-off, and benefit from initial location blurring for enhanced privacy before your driver accepts.

🎨

Culturally Aware Communication & Interface

Experience an app designed with culturally appropriate aesthetics, inclusive imagery, and multi-language support including Urdu, Punjabi, Sindhi, and Pashto. Access respectful quick-reply templates and an in-app glossary for respectful communication.
