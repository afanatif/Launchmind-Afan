Features That Prioritize Your Safety & Identity

👤✍️

Advanced Rider Profile Customization

Specify your preferred name, pronouns, and optionally your gender identity. This crucial information is shared discreetly with your driver during the trip, ensuring you're seen and respected.

✅🛡️

Robust Driver Vetting & Sensitivity Training

Our drivers undergo enhanced background checks, social media reviews, and mandatory LGBTQ+ sensitivity training, focusing on gender identity to guarantee an affirming environment.

🆘📍

Real-time In-App Safety Toolkit

Access an emergency SOS button, share live trip details with trusted contacts, and discreetly report incidents during or after your ride with direct access to LGBTQ+-aware support.

🤝🚗

Optional Driver Matching Preferences

Optionally indicate your preferred driver's self-identified gender during booking. We strive to match you with a driver who aligns with your comfort, balancing preference with availability.

📝🗣️

Pre-Trip Driver Briefing & Notes

Drivers receive a mandatory briefing displaying your preferred name and pronouns, along with sensitivity guidelines. You can also add private notes for specific pickup instructions.
