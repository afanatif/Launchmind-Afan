A food delivery and meal planning platform designed specifically for people with disabilities, providing easy access to dietary restricted meals, convenient ordering, and personalized support

Built for individuals with disabilities, including quadriplegic individuals with limited dexterity, individuals with visual impairments, and caregivers who need to manage meal planning and ordering for their dependents.

Meal Planning and Recommendation Engine

An AI-powered meal planning tool that utilizes natural language processing (NLP) with transformer-based models (e.g., BERT, RoBERTa), collaborative filtering with matrix factorization, and nutritional analysis algorithms (e.g., USDA database) to suggest personalized meal options based on users' dietary restrictions, preferences, and allergies

Accessible Ordering and Payment System

A simple, voice-command enabled ordering system with compatibility for screen readers (e.g., JAWS, VoiceOver), braille displays (e.g., Perkins Brailler), and other assistive technologies, featuring a user-friendly interface with clear and consistent navigation, high contrast colors, and closed captions for video content

Dietary Restriction Filtering

A robust filtering system that allows users to easily find meals that cater to specific dietary needs, such as gluten-free, vegan, low-sodium, halal, kosher, lactose-free, and soy-free, utilizing a faceted search interface and machine learning-based recommendations

Caregiver Support and Management Tools

A set of features that enable caregivers to manage meal planning, ordering, and delivery for their dependents, including shared calendars, notifications, and personalized recommendations

Partnership with Disability-Focused Organizations

Collaborations with organizations such as the Disability Rights Education and Defense Fund, the National Federation of the Blind, and the Autism Society, to provide resources, educational content, and community engagement opportunities
