Inclusive Profile Customization

Allows riders and drivers to specify their chosen name, gender identity, and preferred pronouns. This information is displayed respectfully to the matched party to facilitate correct address.

🔒

Discreet In-App Safety Toolkit

Includes a 'Discreet SOS' button for silent alerts with live location, a low-key call option to emergency services, and 'Share My Ride' for real-time tracking with selected contacts.

🤝

Certified Inclusive Driver Program

Mandatory comprehensive training for all drivers covering transgender sensitivity, respectful pronoun usage, de-escalation techniques, and legal protections. Drivers receive a 'Trans-Ally Certified' badge.

👀

Pre-Ride Driver Transparency

Before confirming, riders can view the matched driver's full 'Inclusive Profile,' including chosen name, pronouns, 'Trans-Ally Certified' badge, and 'Respect & Safety' rating.

✅

Respect & Safety Rating and Reporting

Enables rating on 'Respectful Communication,' 'Sense of Safety,' and 'Adherence to Community Guidelines.' Provides clear reporting for misgendering, discrimination, or harassment with a sensitive support team.
