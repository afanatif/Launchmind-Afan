Inclusive Cricket

Empowering Inclusive Cricket Participation for All

Specialized cricket kits tailored to the unique physical and cognitive needs of differently-abled students

🏏

Adaptive Bat System

A modular cricket bat system featuring adjustable shaft lengths, varied grip diameters, and lightweight composite materials

🎾

Multi-Sensory Cricket Ball

Cricket balls available in various weights and sizes, incorporating internal bells or rattles for auditory tracking and high-contrast patterns for visual tracking

🏆

Stabilized & Adjustable Wicket Set

Wickets with wider, weighted, and anti-slip bases for enhanced stability, featuring adjustable height options and larger, high-contrast bails/stumps

🛡️

Ergonomic & Accessible Protective Gear

Lightweight, easy-donning protective equipment designed with magnetic fasteners, large Velcro straps, or quick-release buckles for ease of use

👍

Assisted Fielding & Ball Retrieval Aids

Long-handled, lightweight ball retrieval scoops or nets to assist students with mobility impairments or difficulty bending

📚

Simple & Intuitive Instructions

Clear, easy-to-follow instructions and user guides for equipment use and cricket rules, designed to support students with intellectual disabilities and learning needs

Who is this for?

Inclusive Cricket is designed for students with diverse needs, including those with intellectual disabilities, physical mobility issues, visual impairments, and other challenges

Our equipment and resources cater to the unique requirements of each student, ensuring equal opportunities for participation and enjoyment of the sport
