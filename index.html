Empowering Features for Your Peace of Mind

🛡️

Rigorous Driver Vetting & Onboarding

Comprehensive multi-point background checks (criminal record, driving history), identity verification, vehicle safety inspections, and mandatory, recurring safety protocol training specifically for female riders.

📍

Real-time Ride Tracking & Trusted Contacts

Riders can designate up to five 'Trusted Contacts' who automatically receive a link to track their live trip status, estimated arrival time, and critical driver/vehicle details on a map, without needing to download the app.

🆘

In-App SOS & Safety Toolkit

An easily accessible, discreet emergency button within the app that, when pressed, silently alerts all Trusted Contacts, simultaneously triggers an immediate call to local emergency services, and initiates a discreet audio recording of the ride.

👩‍✈️

Enhanced Driver & Vehicle Profiles

Before and during a ride, riders can view a detailed profile of their assigned driver, including a clear professional photo, accumulated safety badges, vehicle make/model/color/license plate, and granular safety ratings/feedback specifically from other female riders.

💬

Secure In-App Communication

A secure, anonymized messaging feature for riders and drivers to communicate only within the app. This prevents the exchange of personal phone numbers, offers pre-defined safe messages for common queries, and all communications are logged for safety review.
